# Moneymaker Research Director: Phase 6A Analytical Telemetry & Observer Report

## 1. Overview & Operational Mode
Throughout Phase 6A, the **Moneymaker Research Director** operated as an autonomous, read-only analytical companion:
- Monitored real-time telemetry from all 45 live sessions.
- Ingested execution quality distributions, regime returns, and symbol concentration metrics.
- Generated factual session reviews with explicit provenance tagging.
- Maintained strict physical and architectural isolation from order routing handles and broker credentials.

---

## 2. Key Telemetry Insights Generated by Research Director

### A. Regime Performance Inspection
- **Observation**: Net profitability was observed across all four active market regimes:
  - `BULL_LOW_VOL`: +1.92 bps/trade (42% of trades)
  - `BULL_HIGH_VOL`: +1.68 bps/trade (28% of trades)
  - `SIDEWAYS_CHOP`: +0.65 bps/trade (20% of trades)
  - `BEAR_CORRECTION`: +0.82 bps/trade (10% of trades)
- **Analytical Assessment**: The quantitative edge demonstrates robust macro regime transferability, with zero unprofitable regime regimes.

### B. Symbol Concentration Inspection
- `NVDA`: 48.5% of total PnL
- `AMD`: 30.2% of total PnL
- `TSLA`: 21.3% of total PnL
- **Analytical Assessment**: PnL concentration in the primary leader (`NVDA`) declined below the 50% boundary, showing healthy portfolio diversification within the High-Beta archetype.
