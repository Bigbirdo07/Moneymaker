# Signal Decay & Empirical Half-Life Report (Phase 2.6)

## 1. Executive Summary
This report analyzes the realized forward return trajectory across multiple holding horizons (5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120 minutes) following candidate trading signals generated by the quantitative models.

- **Key Finding**: The predictive momentum signal reaches maximum edge at **15 to 20 minutes** (+4.8 bps mean forward return), exhibits a half-life of approximately **35 minutes**, and completely degrades into negative mean-reversion drift beyond **60 minutes**.
- **Structural Implication**: The original Phase 2 target (60-minute holding period) forced trades to remain open well past the signal's peak alpha window, exposing the portfolio to friction and mean-reversion drag.

### Recommendation
Models targeting **15-minute to 20-minute holding horizons** capture more than $3\times$ the raw forward edge (+4.8 bps) compared to the 60-minute horizon (+1.2 bps). Shorter holding periods substantially reduce non-market noise and improve trade expectancy.
