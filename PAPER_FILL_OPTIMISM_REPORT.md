# Paper Fill Optimism & Execution Realism Report (Phase 3B)

## 1. Executive Summary

Broker paper simulation engines are known to produce optimistic fill pricing by granting instantaneous fills at the inside quote with minimal queue queuing or market impact.

Phase 3B explicitly measures the **Paper Fill Optimism Metric**:
$$\text{Paper Fill Advantage (bps)} = \text{Realistic Shadow Shortfall (bps)} - \text{Broker Paper Shortfall (bps)}$$

- **Mean Paper Advantage**: **+0.46 bps** across all executed trades.
- **Median Paper Advantage**: **+0.38 bps** (P90: +0.82 bps).
- **Materiality Assessment**: The paper optimism (+0.46 bps) accounts for ~29% of the broker paper net edge (+1.58 bps). Crucially, **after stripping out the entire paper advantage, the conservative realistic shadow expectancy remains solidly positive at +1.12 bps/trade**.

## 3. Ground Truth Verdict

Broker paper simulation grants a modest but statistically measurable fill advantage (+0.46 bps). However, because the alpha signal generated by the cross-sectional opportunity ranker (+4.8 bps gross) is sufficiently wide, **the strategy does not rely on paper simulation optimism to be profitable**.
